# Story 1

I wake up. I see the sun.
I am hungry. I look for food.
I walk. I walk. I walk.
I see a tree. Food is under the tree.
I see a cat. The cat is big.
The cat sees me. The cat is angry.
The cat bites. I bite.
The cat scratches. I scratch.
The cat runs.
I walk to the food. I eat the food.
I am full. I am tired.
I see a bush. I walk to the bush.
I lie down. I close my eyes. I sleep.

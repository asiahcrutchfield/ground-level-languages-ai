# Ground Level Languages — Design Philosophy & UI/UX Bible

## 1. Core Pedagogical & Design Mandate
Ground Level Languages is built on an **Acoustics-First, Zero-Literacy** design mandate tailored specifically for adult cognitive acquisition. Every interface element exists to train real-time auditory perception and bypass native language (L1) phonetic filtering before written scripts or grammatical rules are ever introduced.

- **Zero Text, Zero Graphemes, Zero IPA:** Written text, L1 translations, Romanization (Pinyin, Romaji), target scripts (Hán-jī), and phonetic symbols (IPA) are strictly prohibited across the entire user interface.
- **Descriptive Over Prescriptive:** The interface never tells a learner how a sound "should" be produced using textbook tone marks, directional pitch arrows, or numeric scores. Instead, it reflects how native speakers actually articulate sounds in connected speech (including slurring, dropped syllables, and tone sandhi) using neutral, abstract visual categories.
- **Perception Drives Production:** Listening comprehension and binary acoustic discrimination always precede vocal output. When pronunciation diagnostics are active, feedback highlights *where* an articulation diverged without shaming the learner or displaying prescriptive text.

---

## 2. Radical Simplicity & Curiosity-Driven Affordance
Because the interface contains zero written instructions or tutorials, the layout itself must make every interaction immediately obvious through restraint and visual hierarchy:

- **Sparse, High-Contrast Focal Points:** Each screen intentionally presents only a small handful of standout interactive elements (such as the active visual card, the three category switcher buttons, and the tactile shapes on the timeline) surrounded by calm breathing room.
- **Zero Guesswork:** By eliminating menus, settings drawers, toolbars, and visual clutter, learners never hesitate or wonder what to do next—every visible element is interactive and produces immediate auditory feedback.
- **Discovery Through Curiosity:** Instead of explaining features with text tooltips, clean geometric affordances (play triggers, circular category badges, and colored acoustic shapes) naturally draw the user's curiosity to tap, listen, and explore how sounds break down on their own terms.

---

## 3. Visual Aesthetics & Earthy Botanical Palette
To keep cognitive load low and avoid the anxiety of gamified language apps, the visual identity uses a calm, organic **Botanical Earth** palette paired with tactile, non-linguistic shapes:

- **Primary Canvas & Surfaces:**
  - Deep Forest Moss (`#386641` / `#4A7C59`) for primary immersive backgrounds.
  - Warm Sandstone (`#EFECE2` / `#D3CEB6`) for tactile cards, timeline tracks, and subtle progressive-reveal veils.
- **Acoustic Category Signifiers (Non-Prescriptive Shapes):**
  - **Consonants:** Terracotta circles (`●` — `#BC4749` / `#C86D51`).
  - **Vowels:** Sage / Moss triangles (`▲` — `#6A994E` / `#588157`).
  - **Syllable Cadence:** Width-scaled horizontal duration bars proportional to spoken acoustic length.
  - **Pitch & Tonality ($F_0$):** Neutral circular badges linked to stripped vocal humming audio—never directional slashes or pitch contour graphs.
- **Calm, Low-Stress Feedback:**
  - No points, streaks, leaderboards, red warning crosses, or numeric percentages.
  - Diagnostic attention (such as a diverged phoneme or struggled sound) is communicated through a gentle, slow botanical ring pulse.

---

## 4. Interaction & Sensory Rules
1. **Audio-First Exposure:** Learners always hear the native audio clip simultaneously with visual reveal—never seeing an unvoiced image or label first.
2. **Concrete Sensory Primacy:** All Level 0 visuals represent unambiguous physical objects, actions, states, or spatial relationships (photos, minimalist vectors, or looping GIFs) grounded in an animal perspective. Abstract concepts are excluded.
3. **Curiosity-Driven Progressive Reveal:**
   - **Story Preview Carousel:** Upcoming narrative scenes are initially veiled behind translucent botanical obscurers to spark curiosity without spoiling the story arc.
   - **Vocabulary Primer:** Core concrete anchors are primed via direct audio-visual coupling and interactive phoneme/syllable/hum breakdowns before full story playback.
4. **2-Choice Low-Pressure Diagnostics:** Auditory discrimination and L1 filter profiling rely on binary (2-choice) audio/visual selections that measure accuracy and response latency silently in the background to adapt future ear-training drills.
